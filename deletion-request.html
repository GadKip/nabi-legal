Account and Data Deletion Policy for Nabi Last updated: 23-Nov-2026 At Nabi ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your right to control your personal data. In accordance with Google Play policies and applicable privacy laws, this page explains how you can request the deletion of your Nabi account and associated data, as well as what data is deleted or retained. 1. HOW TO REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION You can request the permanent deletion of your Nabi account and personal data by following these steps: Step 1: Send an email from the email address registered to your Nabi account to: support@yourdomain.com Step 2: Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - Nabi" Step 3: Include your full name and the phone number or business ID (H.P./E.M.) associated with your account so we can verify your identity and prevent unauthorized deletion. Once we receive and verify your request, we will process the deletion within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email upon completion. 2. WHAT DATA IS DELETED Upon verification and processing of your request, the following data will be permanently deleted from our active databases: - Your personal user profile (name, email address, phone number, and authentication identifiers). - Personal app preferences, session tokens, and non-statutory draft notes. - Access permissions linking your user account to any business entity within the app. 3. DATA RETAINED FOR LEGAL & TAX COMPLIANCE (IMPORTANT) Because Nabi is an invoicing and business management platform operating under Israeli tax and bookkeeping regulations (הוראות ניהול ספרים): - Statutory Financial Records: Issued tax invoices, receipts, credit notes (Type 320), allocation numbers from the Israel Tax Authority (Shaam), and associated audit logs cannot be deleted upon request. - Retention Period: Under Israeli tax law, businesses and service providers are legally required to retain accounting and tax documentation for a mandatory statutory period (typically 7 years from the end of the relevant tax year). These records will remain securely stored and encrypted solely for legal compliance and auditing purposes, and will no longer be accessible for active use once your account is closed. 4.
