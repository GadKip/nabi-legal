Account & Data Deletion

Compliance with Google Play & Applicable Privacy Regulations

At Nabi, we respect your right to control your personal data. This page provides clear instructions on how you can request the permanent deletion of your Nabi user account and associated personal data, along with explanations of our statutory data retention obligations.

Method 1: Immediate In-App Deletion (Recommended)

Delete Directly Inside the Mobile App:
  1. Open the Nabi app on your device.
  2. Open the Side Menu and navigate to Personal Area (אזור אישי) → My Account (החשבון שלי).
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone (אזור מסוכן).
  4. Tap Permanently Delete Account (מחיקת החשבון לצמיתות).
  5. Confirm your statutory backup acknowledgment and tap to confirm deletion.

Your user profile, authentication credentials, and active permissions will be immediately purged from our servers.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the App or cannot access your device, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

  • Step 1: Send an email from the address registered with your Nabi account to: erastpe@gmail.com.
  • Step 2: Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - Nabi.
  • Step 3: Include your full name and registered business VAT/Company ID (ח.פ / ע.מ) so our team can verify your identity.

Verified requests will be processed within five (5) business days, and you will receive written email confirmation upon completion.

What Data is Deleted

When your deletion request is processed, the following items are permanently erased from active databases:

  • Your personal user profile (first name, last name, email address, phone number).
  • Your Firebase Authentication user identity.
  • App preferences, notification logs, draft documents, and device tokens.
  • User membership and role permissions across all associated businesses.
  • If you are the sole owner of a business entity and confirmed deletion, associated company assets and configuration files are deleted.

Important: Statutory Data Retention (7-Year Bookkeeping Requirement)

Legal Limitation on Deletion: Because Nabi is a certified electronic invoicing platform, tax invoices, receipts, credit notes (Type 320), and official allocation numbers issued through the Israel Tax Authority cannot be wiped immediately upon request. Under Israeli Income Tax Regulations (הוראות ניהול פנקסים), businesses must retain statutory accounting records for seven (7) years. These records will be archived securely, detached from personal profiles, and retained solely to satisfy legal audit obligations.
