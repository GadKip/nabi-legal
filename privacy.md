Privacy Policy for Nabi

Last updated: [16-Nov-2026]

Nabi ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, Nabi (the "App"), collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our invoicing and business management services.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App includes:

- Personal Data (Google Sign-In): When you authenticate or log in using Google Sign-In, we collect your Google account name, email address, and profile identifier to authenticate your identity and set up your user profile.
- Business & Statutory Data: Information required for invoicing and regulatory compliance in Israel, including your business name, legal entity type (e.g., Osek Patur, Osek Murshe, Ltd.), and Israeli VAT ID / Business Registration Number (H.P./E.M.).
- Transaction & Invoicing Data: Details of invoices, receipts, and credit notes you create through the App, including customer names, amounts, tax breakdowns, and transaction dates.
- Automatically Collected Information: Technical information regarding your device, app usage logs, and secure authentication tokens required for secure communication with our backend infrastructure and regulatory gateways.

2. USE OF YOUR INFORMATION

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and legally compliant experience. Specifically, we use information collected via the App to:
- Create and manage your account and business profile.
- Generate, digitally sign, and issue statutory invoices and credit notes.
- Connect with authorized government and tax gateways (such as the Israel Tax Authority / Shaam API) to obtain mandatory invoice allocation numbers as required by local tax reform laws.
- Ensure security, prevent fraudulent activities, and resolve technical issues.
- Comply with applicable legal bookkeeping and tax retention requirements.

3. DISCLOSURE OF YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties, except in the following circumstances:
- Legal & Statutory Compliance (Israel Tax Authority): We transmit necessary transaction data (such as invoice details and business IDs) to the Israel Tax Authority (Shaam) via secure API gateways to comply with mandatory electronic invoicing regulations.
- Service Providers (Cloud & Infrastructure): We use secure cloud infrastructure providers (such as Google Cloud Platform / Firebase) to store and process data securely under strict data protection standards and encryption.
- Legal Obligations: We may disclose your information where required to do so by law, judicial proceeding, court order, or legal process.

4. SECURITY OF YOUR INFORMATION

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal and business information. This includes encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure Firebase Firestore security rules, and cryptographic digital certificate signing (PFX) for issued documents. However, please note that no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed 100% secure.

5. DATA RETENTION

We retain your business records, user profile data, and transaction logs for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services, and to comply with our legal obligations under Israeli tax and bookkeeping laws (which may require retaining financial records for a statutory number of years).

6. YOUR DATA RIGHTS

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored within the App. Because financial documents are subject to strict statutory retention laws, certain tax-related records may need to be retained as mandated by law even upon account closure.
