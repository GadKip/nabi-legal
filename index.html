Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Date: November 2026

Nabi ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile invoicing and business management platform designed for Israeli businesses and freelancers. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal and business data when you use the Nabi mobile application (the "App").

1. Data We Collect & How We Collect It

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety standards, we collect the following categories of data solely to provide app functionality and maintain statutory compliance:

  • Personal Information: First and last name, email address, phone number, and unique account identifiers (Firebase Auth UID). When you authenticate via Google Sign-In, we receive your verified name and email address from Google.
  • Business & Identification Data: Your business legal name, English business name, physical street address, city, and 9-digit Israeli VAT ID / Business Registration Number (ח.פ / ע.מ / ת.ז).
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Generated invoices, receipts, and credit notes, including line items, prices, transaction dates, customer VAT IDs, and optional bank transfer details (bank code, branch, account number) configured for invoice footers.
  • Photos & Files: Business logos uploaded voluntarily via your device photo library to brand your invoices, and generated PDF/ZIP document archives.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Device installation identifiers and crash diagnostics managed through Google Firebase SDKs.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational and statutory purposes:

  • To generate, render, and cryptographically sign (PFX) legal financial documents.
  • To query the Israel Tax Authority (ITA / שע״מ) gateway to obtain statutory Allocation Numbers (מספרי הקצאה) under the Israel Invoices Reform (רפורמת חשבוניות ישראל).
  • To maintain continuous, non-resetting sequential document numbering series as mandated by Israeli bookkeeping regulations.
  • To generate statutory annual tax audit archives (Open Format 1.31 / BKMVDATA.TXT & INI.TXT).
  • To authenticate and manage multi-user team permissions (Owners, Admins, Issuers, Accountants).

3. Third-Party Disclosures & Integrations

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data. Data is shared strictly with the following service providers and regulatory bodies:

  • Israel Tax Authority (ITA / שע״מ): If you issue an invoice subject to or opting into electronic allocation, relevant invoice parameters (amounts, dates, VAT numbers) are transmitted securely to authorized government API servers located in Israel.
  • Cloud Infrastructure (Google Firebase / Google Cloud): Data is stored in secure, encrypted cloud data centers (pinned to the me-west1 Tel Aviv, Israel region) under strict enterprise security rules.
  • Official Israeli Open Data (data.gov.il): Used solely to validate and autocomplete municipal settlement and street names in real time without storing user tracking data.

4. Data Retention & Israeli Statutory Bookkeeping Laws

Statutory Retention Requirement: Under Israeli Income Tax and VAT Regulations (הוראות מס הכנסה בדבר ניהול פנקסי חשבונות), issued tax invoices, receipts, credit notes, and associated audit logs cannot be prematurely purged. Businesses are legally required to retain accounting documentation for seven (7) years from the end of the applicable tax year.

Non-statutory personal data (such as app preferences, notification history, and draft documents) is deleted upon account closure.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, export, or delete your account at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your user account directly inside the App by navigating to Side Menu → Personal Area → My Account → Delete Account.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the App, you may submit a deletion request via our dedicated Account Deletion Page.
